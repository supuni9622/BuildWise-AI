# Vision

Enable anyone to transform vague ideas into build-ready product blueprints using AI consulting teams.

---

# Problem

Most users:

- Cannot express requirements clearly.
- Do not know architecture.
- Do not understand AI tradeoffs.
- Miss security and operational concerns.

As a result:

- Projects fail.
- Overengineering happens.
- Requirements are incomplete.
